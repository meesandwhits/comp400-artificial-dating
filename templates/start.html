The Emergence of “Artificial Dating” and Its Consequences for Offline Connection

McGill COMP 400, Mia Scotter

This project will examine the use of generative AI (such as Rizz, YourMove AI, and other LLMs) across online dating platforms like Tinder and Hinge. I will look at community attitudes toward these tools while focusing primarily on the users themselves. Specifically, I want to understand why people turn to AI for dating advice, how much they rely on it, and if they feel like it's still "them" talking. By conducting user interviews and observing how people use these tools in real time, I will evaluate whether users start depending on AI suggestions instead of trusting their own voice. Ultimately, I want to evaluate how this reliance on AI affects users' real-life social/“dating” skills and explore the broader societal impacts it may have.
